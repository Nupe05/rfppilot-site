Every solicitation starts as noise. Sammie files a flight plan.

RFPpilot is a platform for government-contracting middlemen — the people who connect primes, subs, and set-aside holders to federal work. Sammie is the in-app copilot: it reads the day's postings, tells you which ones fit the partners on your roster, and carries each one from discovery to award.

Early access — accounts by invitation
What Sammie does

Three instruments, one panel

01 · SOURCING

Find & match

Sammie watches SAM.gov and agency forecast lists so you don't have to refresh them by hand.

  • Filters new postings by NAICS code, set-aside type, and contract vehicle
  • Cross-references each posting against your partner roster's capabilities and certifications
  • Sends a same-day digest ranked by fit
02 · CAPTURE

Draft & manage proposals

Sammie turns a matched solicitation into a first-pass proposal package.

  • Builds a compliance matrix straight from Sections L and M
  • Drafts capability statements and past-performance narratives from your library
  • Flags missing volumes and unsigned teaming agreements
03 · PIPELINE

Track every deal to award

Sammie keeps one board for every opportunity you're touching, at any stage.

  • Rolls discovery, teaming, and submission into a single deal record
  • Flags stalled deals and approaching debrief or protest deadlines
  • Logs outcomes back into the matching model
Connected accounts

Sammie works from your inbox and calendar

When you connect your Google account, Sammie can read solicitation-related mail so nothing sits unopened, file proposal drafts and teaming documents to Drive, and add submission and debrief dates to your calendar. Full detail on what's accessed and why is in the privacy policy.
